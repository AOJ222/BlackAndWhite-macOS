Black & White for Mac
=====================

A native macOS port of Black & White (2001) for Apple silicon Macs. It is
an unofficial fan project.

This download contains none of the game's content: the worlds, creatures,
sounds, music, movies and text all come from your own copy of
Black & White, which is read once during set-up and never changed. It does
contain the game's program: the game's executable and program libraries
for each supported version, and Apple silicon code translated from them.
The README in the project repository lists what is in the download.

What you need
-------------
- An Apple silicon Mac running macOS 27. It has been tried on one Mac only
  (an M2 Pro with 16 GB on macOS 27.2); other chips, and macOS 27.0 and
  27.1, are untried.
- Your own copy of Black & White in English: the PC disc as an .iso disc
  image, a folder that is a copy of the disc, or the folder the game is
  already installed in. The original disc, or a copy patched to 1.10, 1.20
  or the unofficial Fan Patch 1.42, can be used (1.10 copies have not been
  tried). Other language editions are not supported yet.
- About 3 GB of free disk space.

Which build you play
--------------------
- An unpatched copy of the game plays the original 1.00 build.
- A patched copy (1.10, 1.20 or the Fan Patch) plays the Fan Patch 1.42
  build.
- Creature Isle (the expansion) is not supported yet.

Set-up (once)
-------------
1. Move "Black & White.app" into your Applications folder.
   ("Black & White Settings.app" too, if you want to change the
   picture size, frame rate limit or sound outside the game.)
2. Open Black & White. macOS may refuse the first launch because the
   app is not notarised: open System Settings, go to Privacy &
   Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway", then "Open".
3. The app asks you to add your copy of the game. Choose your .iso
   image, the folder that is a copy of the disc, or the folder the
   game is installed in. The app checks every file, builds the game
   under ~/Library/Application Support/Black & White, and says when
   it is ready. This takes under a minute.
4. Press "Play Black & White".

After that, just open the app: it goes straight to the game and the
disc is not needed again.

Playing
-------
- Everything is played with the mouse. Control-click (or a two-finger
  click) is the right mouse button; pinch or scroll to zoom.
- Cmd-Q quits. In the game's Options screen you can also change the
  Screen and Resolution while you play, and set Sound and Master Volume.
- "Black & White Settings.app" changes the picture size, frame rate
  limit and sound before you start. It has no volume row; the master
  volume is set in the game's Options screen.
- Saves live in ~/Library/Application Support/Black & White/data.

If something goes wrong
-----------------------
- If the app says it cannot use what you chose, nothing was set up.
  Try the .iso image, a fresh folder copy of the disc, or the folder
  the game is installed in.
- To start set-up over, quit the game and delete the folder
  ~/Library/Application Support/Black & White, then open the app again.
- Logs are kept in ~/Library/Logs/Black & White (the ten newest).

About this port
---------------
The original game's code runs natively on Apple silicon: it was
translated ahead of time with SwARc (https://github.com/JTM-rootstorm/SwARc,
MIT licence). The game's worlds, creatures, sounds, music, movies and text
come only from your copy and are not in the download; the app does contain
the game's program files and code translated from them. Black & White is a
trademark of its owners; this is an unofficial fan port, not affiliated with
or endorsed by them. Please do not share the files imported from your
disc.

The licences of the software in this download are in the Licenses folder
next to the apps. Licenses/SOURCE-OFFER.txt is a written offer of the
source code of FFmpeg, which is under the LGPL.
