Black & White for Mac
=====================

A native macOS port of Black & White (2001) for Apple silicon Macs.

This download contains NO game content. It only works with your own
copy of Black & White, which is read once during set-up and never
changed.

What you need
-------------
- An Apple silicon Mac (M1 or later) running macOS 27.
- Your own copy of Black & White: the PC disc as an .iso disc image, a
  folder that is a copy of the disc, or the folder the game is already
  installed in. Any version works — the original disc as sold, a
  patched copy, or another language edition.
- About 3 GB of free disk space.

New in 0.3.0
------------
- If your copy of the game is patched (1.10/1.20 or the Fan Patch), you
  now play the Fan Patch 1.42 build. An unpatched copy plays the
  original 1.00 build, as before.
- Creature Isle (the expansion) is not supported yet.

Set-up (once)
-------------
1. Move "Black & White.app" into your Applications folder.
   ("Black & White Settings.app" too, if you want to change the
   picture size, frame rate limit or sound outside the game.)
2. Open Black & White. macOS may refuse the first launch because the
   app is not notarised: open System Settings, go to Privacy &
   Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway", then "Open".
3. The app asks you to add your copy of the game. Choose your .iso
   image, the folder that is a copy of the disc, or the folder the
   game is installed in. The app checks every file, builds the game
   under ~/Library/Application Support/Black & White, and says when
   it is ready. This takes under a minute.
4. Press "Play Black & White".

After that, just open the app: it goes straight to the game and the
disc is not needed again.

Playing
-------
- Everything is played with the mouse. Control-click (or a two-finger
  click) is the right mouse button; pinch or scroll to zoom.
- Cmd-Q quits. In the game's Options screen you can also change the
  Screen and Resolution while you play.
- "Black & White Settings.app" changes the picture size, frame rate
  limit and sound before you start.
- Saves live in ~/Library/Application Support/Black & White/data.

If something goes wrong
-----------------------
- If the app says it cannot use what you chose, nothing was set up.
  Try the .iso image, a fresh folder copy of the disc, or the folder
  the game is installed in.
- To start set-up over, quit the game and delete the folder
  ~/Library/Application Support/Black & White, then open the app again.
- Logs are kept in ~/Library/Logs/Black & White (the ten newest).

About this port
---------------
The original game's code runs natively on Apple silicon; the game's
worlds, sounds and pictures come only from your copy. Black & White is a trademark of
its owners; this is an unofficial fan port, not affiliated with or
endorsed by them. No game data is included. Please do not share the
files imported from your disc. Third-party library notices are in
THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md in the project repository.
